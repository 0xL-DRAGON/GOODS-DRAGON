# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all,
regardless of experience level, gender identity, or background.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and kind.
- Do not harass or insult anyone.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect the owner's decisions about the project.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
to the project team at **l.dragon.support@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed
and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate.
